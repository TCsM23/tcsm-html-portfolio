Network Infrastructure of a High School

Apr 28, 2026

I created a Network Infrastructure of my High School. I used Cisco Packet Tracer for it, I designed the network with 1 main router, and 4 helper routers, I divided the school's infrastructure by categorization, I took all public areas and created pools for the public, I took all the internal areas and created pools for the internal part of the school, the ComLabs have their own pools as well. I added FTP, HTTP, and DNS to it to showcase how the network actually flows in packet tracer.
